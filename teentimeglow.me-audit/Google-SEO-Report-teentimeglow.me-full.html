1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of teentimeglow.me for a Lifestyle & Editorial Publisher / Niche Media site, generated on November 01, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO & Infrastructure

Score: 94/100

What Works

  • Strict HTTPS with HSTS (max-age=63072000, includeSubDomains, preload).
  • A+ Security headers: nosniff, DENY frame options, mode=block XSS protection.
  • Edge routing correctly canonicalizes to https://www.teentimeglow.me/ without redirect hops.
  • Robots.txt correctly disallows private endpoints (/admin, /api/) and filtered queries.
  • Clean HTTP 404 responses on non-existent endpoints (no soft-404 catch-alls).
  • Sitemap.xml clean, indexed, and auto-discovered with correct priority weights.

Findings

Declare explicit AI crawler directives in robots.txt Low

Currently all crawlers except AhrefsBot fall through to the wildcard User-agent: * block. Explicit directives for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot clarify crawling vs training boundaries.

Recommendation: Add named User-agent blocks for GPTBot, Claude-SearchBot, and PerplexityBot with Content-Signal declarations.

2.2 Content Quality & E-E-A-T

Score: 90/100

What Works

  • Tested quality scores: Homepage (84/100), Glass Skin Routine (89/100), Capsule Wardrobe (89/100), About Page (91/100), Wallpapers (93/100).
  • Zero AI fluff patterns detected — authentic personal editorial voice by founder Rini.
  • Information density is very high (0.65 to 0.86 across content pages).
  • Explicit editorial disclaimer and non-medical advisory clearly stated.
  • Strong interactive content (Glow Quiz, Daily Quotes, Community Gallery) driving high engagement signals.

Findings

Cite peer-reviewed scientific studies for skincare active ingredients Medium

Guides recommend active ingredients like Niacinamide, Hyaluronic Acid, and Centella Asiatica. Direct external links to scientific research (e.g. PubMed/dermatology journals) elevate E-E-A-T for health-adjacent beauty queries.

Recommendation: Add 2-3 links to PubMed clinical studies in skincare articles when discussing active barrier ingredients.

Include 'Reviewed / Fact-Checked On' timestamps Low

Currently articles show published/updated dates. Adding a visible 'Fact-checked on [Date]' reinforces ongoing freshness for Google quality raters.

Recommendation: Add a micro-byline with verification date near the author bio.

2.3 Structured Data (Schema.org)

Score: 86/100

What Works

  • Extensive schema graphs: WebSite, Organization, Person (Rini), BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, AboutPage, Quotation.
  • Proper entity linking via @id across publisher, founder, and articles.
  • Social sameAs links present for Pinterest and Instagram.

Findings

Missing HowTo schema on step-by-step skincare & styling tutorials High

The Korean Glass Skin guide has defined numbered steps (Double Cleanse, Hydrating Toner, Barrier Serum, Glow Moisturizer, Daily SPF) which qualify for Google HowTo rich snippets.

Recommendation: Add HowTo JSON-LD schema with Step objects, supply requirements, and estimated completion times.

Add FAQPage schema on articles with Q&A sections Medium

Articles answer common teen questions (e.g., routine for acne-prone skin, age suitability).

Recommendation: Markup question-and-answer pairs with FAQPage schema to capture accordion rich results.

Enhance Wallpaper pages with CreativeWork/VisualArtwork schema Low

Wallpaper pages use generic ImageObject. CreativeWork / VisualArtwork provides richer attributes (artMedium, creator, license).

Recommendation: Include CreativeWork schema with license and download links for Google Images authority.

2.4 AI Search (GEO) & Agent Readiness

Score: 82/100

What Works

  • Full server-side rendering: 1,000+ words delivered directly in raw HTML without requiring JavaScript execution.
  • User-triggered AI agents (Claude-User, ChatGPT-User, Perplexity-User) are completely unobstructed.
  • Clean accessibility tree with valid headings, landmarks, and form inputs.

Findings

Missing /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt High

AI models (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude) look for /llms.txt as an authoritative index of high-value resources and site structure.

Recommendation: Create /public/llms.txt with an H1, brief overview, and markdown links to core guides, quizzes, and wallpapers.

Lack of Markdown content negotiation (Accept: text/markdown) Medium

Providing markdown alternatives allows AI retrieval tools to parse articles at fraction of the token cost without HTML clutter.

Recommendation: Serve markdown representations or link rel='alternate' type='text/markdown' versions of key edits.

2.5 Performance & Resource Delivery

Score: 88/100

What Works

  • Preload score: 75/100. Hero LCP image properly prioritized with fetchpriority='high'.
  • BFCache optimization: Zero unload or beforeunload blockers, allowing instant back/forward navigation.
  • Zero console errors during Chromium headless rendering.

Findings

Add Speculation Rules for instant intra-site navigations Medium

Users moving from Home to /edits or /wallpapers must wait for document fetch. Speculation Rules allow the browser to pre-render the target in idle time.

Recommendation: Inject <script type='speculationrules'> to prefetch /edits, /wallpapers, and /quiz on link hover.

Consolidate or self-host external CDN libraries Low

6 external scripts from unpkg, cdnjs, and jsdelivr create separate connection overhead and DNS resolutions.

Recommendation: Bundle Lenis, GSAP, and Lucide through Vite into the client build.

3. Action Plan

Phase 1: Quick Wins & AI Search Readiness Days 1 - 3

  • Deploy /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt at the public root.
  • Add Speculation Rules script tag in the base layout to boost navigation speed to near 0ms.
  • Add explicit AI crawler directives to robots.txt.

Phase 2: Rich Snippet & Schema Expansion Week 1

  • Implement HowTo JSON-LD schema on step-by-step skincare and styling articles.
  • Implement FAQPage JSON-LD schema on articles featuring reader questions.
  • Add CreativeWork/VisualArtwork schema to the 4K wallpapers collection.

Phase 3: E-E-A-T & Authority Reinforcement Weeks 2 - 3

  • Incorporate 2-3 scientific citations (PubMed/dermatological studies) into active ingredient guides.
  • Add 'Fact-checked on [Date]' verification tags to editorial bylines.
  • Promote guides on Pinterest and Instagram with deep links to build external domain citations.

Phase 4: Asset Optimization & Monitoring Month 1

  • Bundle external CDN scripts (GSAP, Lenis, Lucide) directly through Vite.
  • Monitor weekly drift snapshots with claude-seo drift compare.
