## SEO overhaul — TeenTimeGlow

**Diagnosis:** traffic problem was actually a canonical/consolidation problem plus near-zero content (6 articles, one of them 17 words).

**Root causes fixed:**
- Production host is `www.teentimeglow.me`; `teentimeglow.me` 307s to it. Every canonical, og:url, sitemap entry and JSON-LD @id used the non-www host → all canonicals pointed at a redirect. Unified on `www` via new `src/config/seo.ts` (`SITE_URL`, `absoluteUrl()`).
- `public/robots.txt` + `public/sitemap.xml` shadowed the Astro routes — live robots.txt was the stale static copy. Deleted both; routes now win.
- `getPosts()` returned DB rows *instead of* seed articles once any row existed, while `getPostBySlug()` still served seeds → 5 live but completely orphaned articles. Now merges DB + seeds, deduped by slug (DB wins).
- `/edits/[slug]` redirected unknown slugs to `/edits` with 200 (soft 404) → now `Astro.rewrite('/404')`, returns real 404.

**Added:** `/edits/category/[category]` hubs (skincare/fashion/decor), `/about` (E-E-A-T author entity), `/404`, `/rss.xml`, `BreadcrumbList` + `CollectionPage` + `ItemList` + richer `BlogPosting` schema, dynamic sitemap, `noindex` on admin, 308 non-www→www in `vercel.json`.

**Perf:** six third-party scripts deferred; `lucide@latest` pinned to `0.544.0`.

**Strategy doc:** `SEO-STRATEGY.md` — clusters, 90-day calendar, internal-linking rules, KPIs, roadmap.

**Still open (needs user decision):** fabricated social-proof counters on homepage (14,850 readers / 99.4% satisfaction) contradict the About page; no per-wallpaper or per-quote URLs — biggest untapped opportunity.

## First article written — glass-skin-korean-routine

Ran the SEO Content Team end to end. Deliverable: `drafts/glass-skin-korean-routine-2026-10-26.md` (2,151 words), plus `research/brief-glass-skin-routine-2026-10-26.md` and three reports in `reports/`.

**Key finding:** the `posts` table is **empty**. Zero articles live, even though `SEO-STRATEGY.md` describes six. Meanwhile `src/components/Hero.astro:92` and `src/pages/quiz/index.astro:23` both hardcode `/edits/glass-skin-korean-routine` → the homepage's most prominent internal link and every quiz CTA currently 404. The article was written to that exact slug to repair both. **The other three quiz `matchedEditSlug` values still 404** (`ceramide-barrier-repair`, `study-aesthetic-sanctuary`, `autumn-capsule-wardrobe`).

**Two fabricated claims removed from source** (same class as the invented counters, found by the SEO auditor):
- `src/config/seo.ts:69` — skincare hub said "Dermatologist-informed… tested and explained step by step". Now "Straight-talking… explained step by step".
- `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro:91` — the `Person` JSON-LD node said Rini tests routines "before publishing them". Now says "from label reading and research, not from professional qualification". This shipped on every page.

**Template constraint discovered:** `post.title` drives both `<title>` and `<h1>`, and `post.excerpt` drives both `<meta description>` and the visible lede. No separate meta fields. Keep titles ≤45 chars, excerpts ≤160.

**Publish-time traps:** `api/admin/publish.ts:43` hardcodes `read_time_minutes: 3`; `api/admin/posts.ts:47` computes it at /150. `created_at` null falls back to `2026-10-18` in schema. Set all three explicitly or the byline and JSON-LD contradict the body.

**Verified live:** `https://www.aad.org/news/dermatologist-guide-skincare` (AAD, Marisa Garshick MD) independently supports the article's thesis — "consistency matters more than a 10-step routine" — and says teens should avoid retinol. `https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/acne/acne-teenage-five-ways-to-help` supports the scarring and depression points. Both cited in the article.

**Dialect trap worth remembering:** the site's copy is US English, but the strategy doc, the research brief and the first draft all drifted British ("moisturiser", "spots" for pimples, "what needs a GP"). An NHS citation was drafted in and pulled at final review for the same reason. **"spots" is the dangerous one** — it sat in the doctor-referral bullet, where a US teen would not understand it.

**Review round 2 caught what round 1 missed:** the first pass scored 70 (SEO) / 59 (editorial) and still shipped three defects — lowercase "korean" in the lede, a duplicated and ungrammatical safety bullet ("Anything is scarring"), and an NHS link. Lesson: after integrating review feedback, re-run the automated checks (dialect, duplicates, link TLDs) rather than trusting the merge.

## Images integrated

Four AI-generated images, planned in `drafts/glass-skin-image-plan.md`, processed by `scripts/process-glass-skin-images.mjs` (sharp, cover-fit + mozjpeg), verified by `scripts/verify-glass-skin-images.mjs`.

- `glass-skin-cover-202610.jpg` 1200×675 (86 KB), `-order-` / `-morning-` / `-night-` at 1200×800 (165/80/80 KB).
- **Template had zero `img` styling inside `.rich-html-content`** — only `h2`, `p`, `blockquote` were styled. Added `figure` / `figure img` / `figcaption` rules to `src/pages/edits/[slug].astro`. Without them a 1200px image overflows the 614px reading column.
- Article body width is 614px (`1040 − 280 sidebar − 50 gap − 96 padding`), so 1200px exports are ~2x.
- **`/assets/*` is cached `immutable, max-age=31536000`.** Reusing an image filename means the old file sticks for a year. Every image filename must carry a version suffix. Never overwrite.
- Cover must stay exactly **1200×675** because `[slug].astro` hardcodes those numbers in the `ImageObject` schema.

**Open:** the morning image is a masculine-presenting jaw with stubble, in a piece addressed to teen girls and carrying the line "Her skin is not your skin". Flagged to the user as the one image worth regenerating.
