# Plan 10-1: Editorial Magazine Lookbooks & Dynamic Reader Routes (`/edits` & `/edits/[slug]`)

## Objective
Build the dedicated lifestyle and fashion magazine experience:
1. `/edits` — Full magazine catalog index with featured cover story hero, category filters (`Beauty`, `Fashion`, `Decor`), and keyword search.
2. `/edits/[slug]` — High-fashion dynamic reader route with editorial typography, drop caps, pull quotes, author bio, read time, and related articles.
3. Resilient data access via `getPostBySlug(slug)` with instant seed fallback.
