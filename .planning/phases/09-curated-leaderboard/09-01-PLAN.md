# Plan 9-1: Dedicated Curated Leaderboard Standalone Page (`/rankings`)

## Objective
Build an editorial, high-craft standalone page at `/rankings` featuring:
1. Dedicated Prime Video series and Audiobook leaderboard with rank order badges (#1 Golden Hour Pick, #2, etc.).
2. Category switchers (`All Selections`, `Prime Video Binge`, `Addictive Audiobooks`) and genre filters (`Romance`, `Dark Academia`, `True Crime`, `Mindset`).
3. Star rating visualizations, genre pills, and Rini's editorial reviews.
4. Direct streaming/audio sample links with secure attributes (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`).
5. Breadcrumb navigation back to home and responsive mobile-first layout.
