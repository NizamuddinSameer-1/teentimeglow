# Plan 11-1: Dedicated Glow Quiz & Community Hub (`/quiz` & `/gallery`)

## Objective
Build dedicated, standalone pages for TeenTimeGlow's most interactive community features:
1. `/quiz` — An immersive, full-viewport interactive quiz sanctuary where readers discover their aesthetic aura (*Sunset Muse*, *Clean Girl Dew*, *Vanilla Warmth*, *Downtown Indie*), with archetype deep dives, color palettes, and curated recommendation links.
2. `/gallery` — A high-energy community creational dropboard where readers showcase nail art, cozy bedroom setups, DIY crafts, and digital sketches, complete with instant category filters, search, live heart liking (`/api/community/like`), and one-tap modal submission (`/api/community/submit`).
